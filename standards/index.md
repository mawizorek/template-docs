# Standards

The settled answers, and the things people do.

Two types live here and the distinction between them is worth holding onto. A
**standard** is what must be true. A **procedure** is what you do. The same
subject frequently needs both, and collapsing them produces a document that is
bad at being either.

- [Documentation review](@documentation-review) -- a `standard`
- [Rigging inspection](@rigging-inspection) -- a `procedure`
- [Onboarding a new site](@onboarding-a-site) -- a `procedure` with no theatre
  in it at all, which is the one that proves the engine is not secretly
  theatre-shaped
