# Rigging inspection

**Owned by the technical director.** Annually, plus after any structural work,
plus on any reported irregularity in a line set. Covers the counterweight line
sets, the grid, and the loading gallery.

!!! danger "This is an example, not a real inspection regime"

    The content of this page is illustrative. Do not use it as an actual
    safety procedure. A real one is written by somebody qualified, for a
    specific building, and reviewed by somebody who can be held to it.

## Why the owner is named first

A procedure with no named owner does not get done. It gets assumed, and the
assumption survives right up until the moment somebody needed it to have
happened.

That sentence used to justify an `owner:` frontmatter field, which the
`procedure` type drew into a table. The field was removed on 2026-08-03 -- but
note that the argument never actually supported it. "Somebody must be able to
see who owns this" is an argument for a person reading a name on the page, and
a header key is the one place a reader does not look.
