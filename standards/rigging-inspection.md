# Rigging inspection

A worked example of the `procedure` type: a thing somebody does, in an order,
on some occasion.

Compare the generated table above with the one on
[Documentation review](@documentation-review). Different type, different
fields, same treatment. Neither author chose the shape.

!!! danger "This is an example, not a real inspection regime"

    The content of this page is illustrative. Do not use it as an actual
    safety procedure. A real one is written by somebody qualified, for a
    specific building, and reviewed by somebody who can be held to it.

## Why owner is required

A procedure with no named owner does not get done. It gets assumed, and the
assumption survives right up until the moment somebody needed it to have
happened. The type reports a missing owner for that reason and no other.
